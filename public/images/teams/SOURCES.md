# Team logo sources

Original brand artwork, displayed without recolouring or distortion. All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. These images identify teams represented in the portfolio, not endorsements.

- Chevron: [Wikimedia Commons, sourced to Chevron Corporation](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chevron_Logo.svg).
- Lafarge: [Wikimedia Commons, extracted from a Lafarge report](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lafarge_(Unternehmen)_logo.svg). The historical Lafarge mark is retained to match the collaboration name supplied by the portfolio owner.
- JMG: [Official website header logo](https://www.jmglimited.com/assets/jmg-header-r8.webp).
- Total: existing TotalEnergies logo at `/images/fleet-systems/brands/totalenergies.png`.
- Eduvibehub: original `public/Logo V2.png` from the owner's local `eduvibehub` project, also used by that project's navigation and hero.
- Epraise Welding: original `public/assets/logo.png` from the owner's local `epraise_welding` project. Displayed beside its yellow name using the existing mark.
- AutoDrive: original `images/Autodrive_Logo.png` from the owner's local `AutoDrive` project, displayed beside its name.
- BusinessDay: [official website masthead](https://businessday.ng/wp-content/themes/bday_aero/assets/build/images/bd-logo.png), shown only as an affiliation beneath Yaba School of Thought, not as a separate client.

Other teams intentionally retain text labels until their logos are supplied.
